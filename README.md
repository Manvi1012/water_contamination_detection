# Water Contamination Detection System

An **IoT-based water quality monitoring system** designed to measure water safety and contamination levels in real time. This system evaluates critical parameters to determine if water is safe for consumption or usage.

🌟 Features
* **Real-Time Monitoring:** Tracks water conditions continuously.
* **Turbidity Sensing:** Measures the cloudiness or haziness of water caused by large numbers of individual particles.
* **TDS Monitoring:** Calculates Total Dissolved Solids to check the concentration of dissolved substances.
* **Microplastics Detection:** Evaluates the presence of microplastic contamination levels.

## 🛠️ Hardware Requirements
* Microcontroller ESP32 S3
* Turbidity Sensor
* TDS Sensor
* Photodiode

## 💻 Software & Libraries Required
* Arduino IDE (or your preferred IDE)
